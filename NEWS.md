# News

Short release announcements (like `:help news`). Newest first.

## 2026-09-26 — Jewellery Suite v1.0.7 🎉

This release packs the shopkeeper's daily workflow:

- **History** — see every recent collection, given amount and deletion, filter
  by village, with auto-clear retention and an admin-protected "Clear Now".
- **Refinance** — when a customer restructures their loan, the outstanding
  balance rolls into a fresh principal + new interest and the payment schedule
  restarts from today (previous weeks stay visible: on-time / partial / none).
- **Safer deletes** — customers and khatas ask for the admin password before
  deleting, and knock on to their loans + ledger automatically.
- **Clearer numbers** — the profile card now shows Principal | TOTAL PAYABLE |
  Outstanding, and the schedule tiles show partial payments with a
  green/red split bar.

Download: `jewellery_suite_v1.0.7.apk` on the Releases page.
